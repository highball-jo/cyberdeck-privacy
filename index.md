# Cyberdeck Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 27, 2026_

Cyberdeck ("the app") is a retro phone launcher for Android and iPhone
published by JoCoding, Inc. (contact: highball@jocoding.net).

## What stays on your device

Your preferences (screen label, sounds, handset), your melody book and the
numbers you dial stay on your device. They are never uploaded, transmitted or
shared, and are deleted when you uninstall the app. There are no advertising
SDKs and no accounts. The app does send product analytics, described below,
which never include any of these things.

## Permissions we use

- **Phone (CALL_PHONE)** — used only to place a call when you dial a number
  on the retro keypad and press CALL. If you decline this permission, the
  app opens your system dialer instead. Dialed numbers never leave your
  device and are not stored by the app.
- **Internet** — used to send feedback and problem reports when you press
  Send, to send product analytics, and by the store to check purchases.
- **Motion (Android accelerometer / iPhone shake gesture)** — used only to
  notice a shake while Cyberdeck is on screen, which opens "Report a
  problem". Nothing about your movement is stored or sent. You can turn it
  off in Settings ("Shake to report a problem").
- The app reads your device's battery level and signal strength solely to
  draw the retro status bars on screen. This information never leaves your
  device.

## Feedback

Nothing is sent unless you open **Send feedback** and press Send. Then we
receive:

- the message you typed;
- the screenshot, only if you chose to attach one;
- your email address, only if you ticked "OK to contact me" and typed it;
- your app version, device model and language;
- a random identifier created on first launch, which lets us recognise
  repeated messages from the same install. It is not linked to your Apple or
  Google account, your name, or any advertising identifier, and it is
  forgotten when you delete the app.

Feedback is stored on Cloudflare (our server provider) and read only by the
people who make Cyberdeck. Attached screenshots are deleted automatically
after 90 days. To have a message deleted sooner, email us.

## Report a problem

You can report a problem from Settings, from the "Report" button on an error,
or by shaking your phone. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. Then we
receive what you wrote; a screenshot of the screen you were on when you
shook or tapped (attached by default, shown to you before sending, and
removable with one tap); and app version and build, operating system version,
device model, language, the name of the screen you were on, and the last
error code the app recorded. No logs and nothing else from your phone. It is
stored and deleted like feedback, above.

## Short questions

Now and then (at most one question per visit, and never more often than every
two weeks) the app may ask one short question, such as how you would feel
without Cyberdeck, and on first launch it may ask what you will use it for and
how you heard about it. Every question can be skipped. The option you tap is
sent with product analytics (below). Anything you choose to type is sent to
our feedback server, like feedback, and never to analytics.

## Product analytics

We use PostHog (servers in the United States) to understand how the app is
used, so we can improve it. The app sends named events with simple values:
for example that a call was placed or an app opened from the menu (never
which number or which app), that a handset was changed, the steps of a
purchase, and the option you tapped on a question. Events never include
phone numbers, names, your melody book, your screen label, anything you type,
or screenshots. They are linked to the pseudonymous identifier RevenueCat
assigns to your purchases (or, without one, a random identifier PostHog
creates), not to your name or account. PostHog uses your IP address to
estimate an approximate location (country and city) and does not keep the
address itself. See https://posthog.com/privacy.

### Session replay

The app contains PostHog session replay, and it is **currently switched off**.
If we switch it on, it may record about one in ten visits in which the handset
shop is opened, to see where buying gets confusing. A recording shows how
screens are moved through and tapped, with **all text, images and typed input
masked**. Screens that show your installed apps, a number you are dialing,
your melody book, or a message you are writing are never recorded: opening
one stops the recording for the rest of the visit. Recording stops when you
leave the app. No logs and no network data are recorded.

## In-app purchases

If you buy a handset pack, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google. The
app uses RevenueCat to validate purchase entitlements; RevenueCat receives a
pseudonymous purchase token and no personal identity data. See RevenueCat's
privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy for details.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no name, contact details or
other identity data from anyone unless they choose to give them in feedback.
