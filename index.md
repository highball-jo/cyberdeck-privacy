# Cyberdeck Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 25, 2026_

Cyberdeck ("the app") is a retro phone launcher for Android and iPhone
published by JoCoding, Inc. (contact: highball@jocoding.net).

## What stays on your device

Your preferences (screen label, sounds, handset), your melody book and the
numbers you dial stay on your device. They are never uploaded, transmitted or
shared, and are deleted when you uninstall the app. There are no analytics,
no advertising SDKs, and no accounts.

## Permissions we use

- **Phone (CALL_PHONE)** — used only to place a call when you dial a number
  on the retro keypad and press CALL. If you decline this permission, the
  app opens your system dialer instead. Dialed numbers never leave your
  device and are not stored by the app.
- **Internet** — used only to send feedback when you press Send in the
  feedback sheet, and by the store to check purchases.
- The app reads your device's battery level and signal strength solely to
  draw the retro status bars on screen. This information never leaves your
  device.

## Feedback

Nothing is sent unless you open **Send feedback** and press Send. Then we
receive:

- the message you typed;
- the screenshot, only if you chose to attach one;
- your email address, only if you ticked "OK to contact me" and typed it;
- your app version, device model and language;
- a random identifier created on first launch, which lets us recognise
  repeated messages from the same install. It is not linked to your Apple or
  Google account, your name, or any advertising identifier, and it is
  forgotten when you delete the app.

Feedback is stored on Cloudflare (our server provider) and read only by the
people who make Cyberdeck. Attached screenshots are deleted automatically
after 90 days. To have a message deleted sooner, email us.

## In-app purchases

If you buy a handset pack, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google. The
app uses RevenueCat to validate purchase entitlements; RevenueCat receives a
pseudonymous purchase token and no personal identity data. See RevenueCat's
privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy for details.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects nothing from anyone unless they
choose to send feedback.
