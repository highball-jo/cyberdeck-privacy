# Cyberdeck Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 19, 2026_

Cyberdeck ("the app") is a retro launcher for Android and a retro phone experience for iOS published by jocoding
(contact: highball@jocoding.net).

## Data we collect

**None.** Cyberdeck does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
data. There are no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no accounts.

## Permissions we use

- **Phone (CALL_PHONE)** — used only to place a call when you dial a number
  on the retro keypad and press CALL (on iOS the call goes through the system dialer). If you decline this permission, the
  app opens your system dialer instead. Dialed numbers never leave your
  device and are not stored by the app.
- The app reads your device's battery level and signal strength solely to
  draw the retro status bars on screen. This information never leaves your
  device.

## Data stored on your device

Your preferences (screen label text, sound on/off, selected theme) are
stored locally on your device only, and are deleted when you uninstall the
app.

## In-app purchases

If you buy a theme, the purchase is processed by Google Play. When purchases
are enabled, the app uses RevenueCat to validate purchase entitlements;
RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous purchase token and no personal identity
data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
for details.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone,
including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with
